Number Systems in Computer Architecture

Digital hardware operates using logic gates where voltage levels represent binary bits (0 and 1). Higher radixes provide human-friendly shorthands for byte mapping:

  • Binary (Base-2): Fundamental unit of storage (bit, byte, word).
  • Octal (Base-8): Direct mapping to 3-bit sets ($2^3 = 8$).
  • Hexadecimal (Base-16): Direct mapping to 4-bit nibbles ($2^4 = 16$), memory addresses.
  • Decimal (Base-10): Everyday human positional representation.

Conversion Algorithms & Formulas

Standard ALU methods for radix conversion:

  • Radix $B \to$ Decimal: $N = \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} d_i \cdot B^i$.
  • Decimal $\to$ Radix $B$: Successive division by $B$; read remainders MSB $\to$ LSB.
  • Binary $\leftrightarrow$ Hex: Partition bits into 4-bit nibbles from right to left.
  • Binary $\leftrightarrow$ Octal: Partition bits into 3-bit triplets from right to left.
